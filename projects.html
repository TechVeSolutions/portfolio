01

The cable

Networks, by hand.

  • 2024–2025 · Field

    Structured cabling installs

    Homes and offices needed reliable wired networks. I pulled, dressed, terminated and certified Cat5e to Cat8 copper runs and patch panels, which cut downtime by 15%.

  • Ongoing · Network

    Segmented home lab

    I wanted a safe place to test before touching a real network. I run a Zyxel USG FLEX 50H firewall and a Cisco CBS350 managed switch with separate VLANs and a VPN.

02

The office

Keeping the network honest.

  • Aug 2025 · Network

    Firewall administration on Palo Alto NGFW

    A flat network lets one infected machine reach everything. On a VM-Series firewall I built zones, security policies, NAT rules and custom URL filtering to segment it.

  • Aug 2025 · Network

    Attack traffic analysis with Wireshark

    A packet capture held an attack in plain sight. I followed the TCP handshake through HTTP and DNS traffic, filtered the noise and pulled out the stolen credentials.

  • Aug 2025 · Network

    Windows Firewall log analysis

    Logs only help if someone reads them. I went through Windows Firewall logs, read the TCP flags and documented port scans and suspicious single-port connections.

  • Jul 2025 · Network

    SSL certificate analysis

    Every secure website depends on a chain of trust. I took a certificate apart: its fingerprints, the chain up to the certificate authority and the revocation list.

03

The shield

Finding it, fixing it, reporting it.

  • Sep 2025 · Security

    Windows forensics investigation

    A Windows machine was compromised and nobody knew how. With Event Viewer, CMD and VirusTotal I rebuilt the timeline: the account used, the processes and the malicious activity.

  • Oct 2025 · Security

    NotPetya incident report

    Leaders need to know what happened and what it cost. I rebuilt the timeline of the attack on Maersk, sized the infected assets and wrote a full incident report with an executive summary.

  • Oct 2025 · Security

    Vulnerability management with Nessus

    Scan results mean nothing without priorities. I scanned systems, ranked findings from Critical to Low with CVSS and wrote a remediation report with clear next steps.

  • Sep 2025 · Security

    Vulnerability program analysis

    An organization had more findings than time. I went through its scan data to find the highest-risk systems, the most critical issues and the oldest unpatched vulnerabilities.

  • Sep 2025 · Security

    EDR evaluation

    A business had to choose endpoint protection. I compared three EDR products against its requirements with risk-control mapping and recommended the best fit.

  • Sep 2025 · Security

    Phishing email analysis

    Five suspicious emails, one question: which ones are phishing? I checked every link in VirusTotal, classified each email and documented its indicators of compromise.

  • Jul 2025 · Security

    Cryptography with CyberChef

    To defend encryption you have to understand it. I practiced hashing, encoding, encryption and cryptanalysis, and solved cipher puzzles including MD5.

  • Jul 2025 · Security

    AI in offense and defense

    Attackers already use AI, so defenders should know how. I tested ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot on phishing simulation, keylogger awareness and defensive guidance, and compared the results.

  • Oct 2025 · Compliance

    GDPR audit of a messaging app

    Privacy policies make promises the law can check. I reviewed a major messaging app's policy against GDPR: data security, retention, international transfers, cookies and data subject rights.

  • Oct 2025 · Compliance

    ISO 27001:2022 audit checklist

    An audit is only as good as its questions. I wrote an ISO 27001:2022 checklist for data security, asset management and risk assessment, with five control questions per area.

These labs were part of the Post Graduate Program in Cybersecurity at UT Austin McCombs School of Business (conferred January 2026), plus my own practice at home.
