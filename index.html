01

The cable

I learned networks by pulling the cable.

My first IT job was in the field. As an installation specialist at ITC Service Group, I ran fiber and Ethernet into homes and businesses. I terminated copper, set up ONTs and routers, and tested every run before I left. I also showed customers where to put the router and how to keep it secure. Plain words, no jargon. I still work that way.

  • Installation Specialist, ITC Service Group · Jul 2024 – Dec 2025
  • Fiber optic and Ethernet installs, terminations and repairs
  • Cat5e/6/6a/7/8 cabling, keystone jacks, patch panels, RJ45
  • ONTs, routers and network equipment set up for each client
  • Cable runs tested and certified before sign-off
  • Clear service records and work orders on every job

02

The office

Then I kept an office running.

At Exacta Bookkeeping & Tax Services I was the IT administrator for 30+ people across 4 offices. I managed Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, enrolled devices in Intune and kept Hyper-V machines running. In tax season, downtime is not an option. So I fixed things fast, and I read the logs to catch problems before anyone called.

  • IT Administrator, Exacta Bookkeeping & Tax Services · Dec 2025 – Apr 2026
  • Microsoft 365 and Entra ID: users, security groups, licenses, access policies
  • Microsoft Intune: device compliance and company-wide app deployment
  • Hyper-V virtual machines for testing and business continuity
  • Onboarding and offboarding: accounts, devices, and access removed on the way out
  • Windows 10/11 and macOS support, plus QuickBooks, Drake Tax and PrintBoss
  • Microsoft Universal Print across locations, with no on-site print servers
  • 10+ support tickets a day, tracked in Zoho Desk

03

The shield

Then I learned to defend it.

Running real systems showed me how much can go wrong. So I went deep on security. I finished the Post Graduate Program in Cybersecurity at UT Austin McCombs School of Business in January 2026. Now I'm working on a B.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance at WGU. The program was built on hands-on labs. These are six I'm proud of.

  • Post Graduate Program in Cybersecurity · UT Austin McCombs School of Business · Conferred Jan 2026 · Verify
  • B.S. Cybersecurity and Information Assurance · Western Governors University · 2026–2028

  • Firewall administration on Palo Alto NGFW

    Set up security policies, NAT rules, URL filtering and zones on a VM-Series firewall.

  • Vulnerability management with Nessus

    Scanned systems, ranked findings by severity and wrote a remediation report.

  • Attack traffic analysis with Wireshark

    Followed HTTP and DNS captures through the TCP handshake and pulled out stolen credentials.

  • NotPetya incident report

    Rebuilt the timeline of the attack on Maersk and wrote a full report on scope and recovery.

  • Windows forensics investigation

    Traced a compromise with Event Viewer and CMD: the timeline, the account, the malicious activity.

  • EDR evaluation

    Compared three EDR products against business needs with risk-control mapping and recommended the best fit.

At home I run a segmented network: a Zyxel USG FLEX 50H firewall and a Cisco CBS350 managed switch, with VLANs and VPN.

Next up: CompTIA Security+, Network+ and A+, Cisco CCNA, and Microsoft Azure and AWS certifications.
